I am a Scott-Titterington Research Fellow in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Glasgow. In December 2026, I will join the Département de mathématiques at the Université du Québec à Montréal (UQAM) as an Assistant Professor.
My research develops statistical methods for questions involving social networks, causal effects and complex sampling. I work on causal inference under network interference, confounding and homophily, as well as survey methodology and longitudinal studies of hard-to-reach populations. Many of these questions arise in population health and education.
I completed my PhD in Biostatistics at McGill University in 2024, following an MSc in Statistics at the Université de Montréal.
